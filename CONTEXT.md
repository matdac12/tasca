# Tasca

A Windows file shelf and clipboard history: park files on the Shelf and drag them out later, or open the History and paste something copied earlier.

## Shelf

**Shelf**:
The compact list on the left edge of the screen that holds parked files.

**Item**:
One row on the Shelf: a file, a folder, or a Stack of files parked together.
_Avoid_: Clip, entry

**Park**:
Drop something onto the Shelf.

**Take out**:
Drag an Item off the Shelf into another app.

## Clipboard history

**History**:
The list of past clipboard contents, newest first, opened with Alt+V.
_Avoid_: Clipboard manager, clipboard list

**Clip**:
One entry in the History: what was copied at one moment, as text (with its formatting), an image, or a list of copied files.
_Avoid_: Item, entry, snippet

**Pinned Clip**:
A Clip kept at the top of the History and never pushed out by newer Clips or cleared.
_Avoid_: Saved clip, favourite

**Private copy**:
A copy the source app marked "do not record" (password managers do). Skipped by default; when the user allows them, it becomes an ordinary Clip.
_Avoid_: Secret, sensitive clip
