Each variant: the whole screen first (where it opens, shrunk), then the panel at true size. The panels are live: click into the search, then type, ↑ ↓, Enter or click to paste (Shift = plain text), Alt/Ctrl+1…9, Ctrl+P pin, Ctrl+Del delete, Esc, right-click a row.

A · At the cursor

Like ClipClip's paste menu and Maccy: opens at the mouse pointer, moved to stay on screen. 380 wide, about nine rows, one-line text plus a type line. Closest to your habit, but the mouse is often nowhere near where you are typing.

True size

B · Centre palette

Like Raycast or PowerToys Run: big and centred in the top third of the monitor the mouse is on. Large search, taller rows, Alt 1 hints and the keys in the footer. Always in the same place and easy to read; the furthest from ClipClip.

True size

C · Bottom strip

Like Paste on the Mac: cards along the bottom of the screen with big text excerpts and real image previews. Only six or seven Clips show at once, and ← → have to move the selection, which fights the search caret.

True size (scroll sideways)

D · Left edge, the Shelf's sibling

Same place and look as the Shelf, a little wider, with a search line under the header. Tasca feels like one app; but it opens away from where you type, and it shares the left edge with the Shelf.

True size
